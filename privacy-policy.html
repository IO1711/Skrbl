Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data. Skrbl does not require an account and does not send information to servers controlled by us.

Nearby multiplayer

When you join a nearby game, Skrbl uses your device’s local network or Bluetooth connection to communicate directly with other devices in that game. Session information—such as a display name, game activity, and drawings—may be visible to the other participants in the room while you play.

This information is used only to provide the multiplayer session. We do not receive it, store it on an external server, or share it with third parties. Session information is temporary and is not retained by Skrbl after the session ends.

Camera, local network, and Bluetooth

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a room QR code. The camera image is processed on your device and is not saved or uploaded by Skrbl.
  • Local network: used to discover and connect to nearby game rooms on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • Bluetooth: used to discover and connect to nearby game rooms when you choose Bluetooth play.

You can control these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings app.

Data storage and third parties

Skrbl does not store your personal data, use cookies or tracking technologies, include advertising, or use third-party analytics. We do not sell, rent, or disclose personal information to third parties because we do not collect it.

Children’s privacy

Skrbl does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. If you believe you have a privacy concern, please contact us using the support information on this page.

Changes to this policy

If Skrbl’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the new effective date will be shown above.
